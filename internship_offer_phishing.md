# 🎓 Fake Internship Offer (Advance-Fee + Identity Theft)

## Scenario
This phishing email targets students with a fake internship offer, requesting personal documents and an upfront fee.

## Sample Email
From: HR Recruitment <careers@internship-global.org>
Subject: Internship Opportunity — Microsoft Partner Program

Dear Luxolo,

Congratulations! You have been shortlisted for a prestigious internship with our Microsoft Partner Program.
This opportunity provides international exposure and a monthly stipend of R2,000.

To proceed, please send:

Copy of your ID or passport

Updated CV

Proof of payment (R100 processing fee) via bank transfer to:
Account Name: Internship Global
Bank: First National Bank
Account No: 123456789

Deadline: Submit documents within 48 hours to secure your placement.

We look forward to welcoming you to the program.
HR Recruitment Team

## 🔎 Annotations
- **Advance-fee fraud:** Requests $50 "processing fee" upfront.  
- **Identity theft:** Asks for ID/passport and CV, which can be misused.  
- **Student targeting:** Tailored to young job seekers, exploiting career aspirations.  
- **False legitimacy:** Mentions "Microsoft Partner Program" to appear credible.  

## ✅ Key Takeaway
Legitimate internships never ask for upfront fees or sensitive documents via email — treat such requests as scams.
